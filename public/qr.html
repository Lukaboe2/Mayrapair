New User Guide — Step by Step
1
Pick your Session Type
Choose before generating — you can't change it after.
SHORT A compact ID like GURU~aBc123XyZ — requires a database on this server.
LONG A full self-contained string — works anywhere, no database needed. Recommended for beginners.
2
Click "Generate QR Code"
A fresh QR code will appear on screen. It expires in about 60 seconds — scan it quickly. If it expires, go back and generate a new one.
3
Scan with WhatsApp on your phone
Open WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device → point your camera at the QR code.
On iPhone Settings → Linked Devices → +
On Android ⋮ Menu → Linked Devices → Link a Device
4
Receive your Session ID on WhatsApp
After a successful scan, the bot will send your Session ID directly to your own WhatsApp chat. Copy it and paste it into your bot's config file.
